About us

Building homes in Alappuzha with trust and care

Construction, contract works and interior design, handled by one team from the first plan to the final finish.

Who we are

BISMI Construction is based at Civil Station, Alappuzha. We build residential and commercial projects and design interiors, combining practical engineering with careful craftsmanship.

Our engineers, designers and site teams work together on every project. Because design, construction and interiors sit with one contractor, decisions are made quickly and the finished building matches the plan you approved.

Before work begins, we prepare 3D views of the building and its rooms so you can see the result, make changes on paper and build with confidence.
