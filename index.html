How a project runs

From site visit to handover

You talk to the same team at every stage, so nothing gets lost between the designer and the site.

  1. Consultation

    We visit the site, listen to what you need and talk through the budget.

  2. Design & 3D views

    Plans and 3D views, so you see the building before it is built.

  3. Structural planning

    Engineers finalise the structure, materials and schedule.

  4. Construction

    Our supervisors manage materials and manpower on site.

  5. Interiors & handover

    Finishes and interiors are completed, then the keys are yours.
