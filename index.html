How a project runs

From site visit to handover

You talk to the same team at every stage, so nothing gets lost between the designer and the site.

  1. Consultation

    We visit the site, listen to what you need and talk through the budget.

  2. Design & 3D views

    Plans and 3D views, so you see the building before it is built.

  3. Structural planning

    Engineers finalise the structure, materials and schedule.

  4. Construction

    Our supervisors manage materials and manpower on site.

  5. Interiors & handover

    Finishes and interiors are completed, then the keys are yours.

About us

Building homes in Alappuzha with trust and care

BISMI Construction is based at Civil Station, Alappuzha. We build residential and commercial projects and design interiors, combining practical engineering with careful craftsmanship.

Because design, construction and interiors sit with one contractor, decisions are made quickly and the finished building matches the plan you approved.

Mission

To deliver high-quality construction and interior solutions that exceed client expectations through professionalism, integrity and innovation.

Vision

To become Kerala's most trusted construction brand by providing modern, sustainable and affordable construction services while building long-lasting relationships with our clients.

Quality work

On-time delivery
